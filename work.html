Nookcha Matcha

My own concept

Brand concept · Menu · Sourcing

Concept

The idea

A small, community-minded matcha pop-up. The name plays on nokcha, Korean for green tea, and a “nook”: somewhere cozy you’d want to sit for a while. The feel I’m going for is minimal, warm, and homelike.

Where it stands

  • A planned menu of straight matcha plus strawberry, blueberry, and French vanilla matcha lattes.
  • Keicha Matcha as the intended supplier, the same matcha I worked with at Retreat Sweets.
  • I’m treating it as a hands-on way to practice branding and launch planning, rather than a business I need to profit from right away.

A desktop cat that reminds you to take breaks

Personal Mac app

Learning to code

In progress

The idea

A small cat that walks around your screen. You can pick him up, move him, and pet him. A settings app lets you set a timer, so he shows up every so often to remind you to step away, and lets you change how he looks.

Why I’m building it

Mostly to learn how apps are actually made. It was inspired by Workcat, a desktop cat that closes short-form video apps; I picked a simpler timer version I could finish.
