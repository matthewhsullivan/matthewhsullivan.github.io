Featured · Fall 2026

Getting people to show up for UConn’s Korean Student Association

As co-public relations chair of UConn’s Korean Student Association, I co-run our Instagram and TikTok and write the copy for every video we post. The goal is simple: make a student stop scrolling long enough to put the event in their calendar.

These two promoted a spooky scavenger hunt (sponsored by Weee!) and a one-day Korean convenience-store pop-up in the Student Union. Both star real members in short skits instead of a flyer read aloud.
