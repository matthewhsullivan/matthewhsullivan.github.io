Featured · Fall 2026

Getting people to show up for UConn’s Korean Student Association

As co-public relations chair of UConn’s Korean Student Association, I co-run our Instagram and TikTok and write the copy for every video we post. The goal is simple: make a student stop scrolling long enough to put the event in their calendar.

These promoted a spooky scavenger hunt, a one-day Korean convenience-store pop-up in the Student Union, and Peperocky Night, a joint event with UConn’s Japanese Student Association. Two were sponsored by the grocery app Weee!, and all three star real members in short skits instead of a flyer read aloud.
