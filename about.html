A bit about me

How I got here

I grew up in Fairfield, Connecticut, and I have Korean roots. I started college at UConn Stamford, where I cofounded the Investment Banking Club and ran its marketing, then transferred to the main campus in Storrs. I’m a junior now, studying marketing, and I graduate in May 2028.

Last May I went to Brazil with UConn’s BCLC program and worked with students from other countries on a case for a real consulting client. Our team took first place. This fall I joined the Korean Student Association as co-PR chair, which is where most of my video work happens now.

What I like working on

The problems I enjoy sit where people and business meet. Why a shopper picks one thing over another. Why a student skips an event they’d probably enjoy. What makes someone stop scrolling for three seconds.

I learn by asking questions, usually more than I strictly need to. Curiosity is the trait I lean on most, whether I’m interviewing a client, digging into a competitor, or figuring out a new editing trick.

Me and technology

I’m interested in generative AI and in using it to take the repetitive parts out of work. On the technical side, I work with HTML and Python, and I’m building up my SQL.
