THE PROBLEM
Client
engagement
is broken.
Profile's pre-client process is slow, manual, and disconnected. Before the first call, hours are already wasted and insights keep slipping through.
Hours of manual research
Planners dig through client websites one by one with no structure, no templates, and no consistency across the team.
Meeting insights get lost
Notes are scattered, transcriptions are informal, and key client goals disappear between the call and the proposal.
Delivery is slow and fragmented
Strategy, creative concepts, and next steps live in separate tools. The client waits days for a response that should take hours.
OUR SOLUTION
A 4-Phase AI System for Client Engagement
PHASE 01
Pre-Meeting Research
AI scrapes the client's website before any call is scheduled, generating a full company brief automatically.
PHASE 02
Live Meeting and Transcription
AI joins the call as a silent participant, transcribing everything and tagging goals, pain points, and action items in real time.
PHASE 03
Internal Dashboard
Research and transcript flow into one workspace with strategy, AI art, next steps, and monday.com task creation.
PHASE 04
Client Dashboard
A polished, branded deliverable sent directly to the client with visuals, strategy, a calendar, and a one-click Accept button.
Fully integrated from the first search to the client's signed approval.
